Types of casino bonus

Deposit match

The casino adds a percentage of your deposit as bonus money, for example 100% up to $500. The bonus is locked until you meet the wagering requirement.

Free spins

Spins on a named slot at a fixed stake. Winnings usually land as bonus money with their own wagering. Check the spin value: 100 spins at $0.10 is $10 of play.

Cashback

A percentage of net losses returned, often weekly. Low-wager cashback is the most honest bonus type because it only pays when you've already lost, and it doesn't push you to bet more.

Reloads and tournaments

Smaller matches on later deposits and leaderboard prizes. They are designed to keep you depositing, so only take them if you were going to play anyway.

Five terms that matter more than the headline amount

  1. Wagering multiplier and whether it covers the bonus alone or deposit plus bonus. 35x on deposit plus bonus is double the bets of 35x on the bonus alone.
  2. Game weighting. Slots usually count 100%; blackjack and roulette often count 10% or nothing.
  3. Maximum bet while the bonus is active, commonly around $5. Going over it can void winnings.
  4. Expiry. Seven days to bet $3,500 is a lot of play. Short deadlines push you to gamble faster.
  5. Maximum cashout on free-spin or no-deposit winnings.
