Safety and player protection

Betory is reported to be licensed in Costa Rica, not by a Canadian province. Costa Rica is a lighter licence than Curaçao or Malta, with no dedicated gambling regulator to take complaints. If a dispute can't be settled with support, you have few places to escalate it. Player reviews on Trustpilot are mixed to negative, and most complaints are about withdrawals and account checks. Keep screenshots of the bonus terms and your chat history, deposit only what you can afford to lose, and withdraw winnings promptly. Reviewers found few self-service limit tools, so ask support to set a deposit limit before you start.
