Online casinos in British Columbia: PlayNow, offshore sites and the law

British Columbia has one government online casino, PlayNow, run by BCLC. Offshore casinos also accept B.C. players. Here's how the two compare and what the 2026 changes mean for you.

The law in B.C.

  • Legal age: 19.
  • Provincial site: PlayNow.com, operated by the British Columbia Lottery Corporation (BCLC).
  • New regulator: B.C.'s Gaming Control Act came into force on April 7, 2026 and created the Independent Gambling Control Office, separate from BCLC.
  • Private online licensing: not yet. Unlike Ontario and Alberta, B.C. has no system for licensing private online casinos, and commentators don't expect one soon.

Offshore casinos licensed in places like Curaçao accept B.C. players, but they're outside the province's oversight. That affects disputes and self-exclusion, as the table shows.

Getting help in B.C.

The B.C. Problem Gambling Help Line is free, confidential and open 24 hours: 1-888-795-6111. BCLC's GameSense programme also has advisers at B.C. casinos and on PlayNow. If you use offshore sites as well, exclude from each one separately; a PlayNow self-exclusion doesn't reach them.

FAQ

Is online gambling legal in British Columbia?

Yes, through BCLC's PlayNow. Offshore casinos also accept B.C. players but aren't regulated by the province.

What is the gambling age in B.C.?

19.

Will B.C. license private online casinos?

Not yet. The Gaming Control Act that took effect on April 7, 2026 created a new independent regulator but didn't open a private online market.
