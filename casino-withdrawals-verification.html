Casino withdrawals and account verification: get paid without delays

Most withdrawal complaints come down to one thing: the player tried to cash out before the casino had verified who they are. Do it in the first week and payouts become routine.

Why casinos verify you

Licensed casinos, offshore ones included, must confirm your age, identity and address, and that the payment method is yours. These "know your customer" (KYC) checks prevent underage play, fraud and money laundering. They aren't optional, and the casino will hold withdrawals until they're done.

How long a withdrawal takes

  1. Pending period. Many casinos hold a request for up to 24 to 72 hours before processing. During this time you can often cancel it. Don't, unless you meant to; reversed withdrawals are a classic route to losing winnings.
  2. Review. The finance team checks bonus terms, KYC status and the payment method.
  3. Transfer. E-wallets and crypto are usually fastest after approval. Interac and bank transfers often take 1 to 3 business days.

Offshore casinos also set daily, weekly or monthly withdrawal caps. A large win may be paid in instalments. Check the limits in the terms before you play for high stakes.

Mistakes that freeze payouts

  • Using a VPN or playing from a restricted province. Casinos can cancel winnings from places they don't accept, including Ontario and Alberta for the casinos we review.
  • Different names on your casino account and your bank or card.
  • Breaking bonus rules, especially the maximum bet while a bonus is active.
  • Opening a second account at the same casino or a sister brand to claim another welcome bonus.
  • Withdrawing to a different method than you deposited with, when the casino requires the same method.

If a withdrawal is refused

Ask support for the exact rule they're relying on, in writing. Save your chat logs, the bonus terms you accepted and screenshots of your balance. If that fails, the casino's licence page names its regulator and complaints process. Offshore casinos aren't covered by Canadian dispute services, which is one reason to keep balances small and withdraw regularly.

FAQ

How long does it take to verify a casino account?

Usually 24 to 72 hours once clear documents are uploaded. Blurry photos and old utility bills are the most common reasons for delays.

Is it safe to send my ID to an online casino?

Licensed casinos are required to collect it. Upload through the casino's account page, never by email or chat links, and cover the middle digits of any card.

Why is my withdrawal still pending?

Most casinos hold requests for a pending period, then review them. Unfinished KYC, an active bonus or a payment-method mismatch can add time.
