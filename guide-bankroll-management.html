Bankroll Management

Bankroll management can't beat the house edge. What it can do is make sure gambling stays a cost you planned for, not a problem you discover later.

Step 1: set a monthly gambling budget

Use money left after bills, savings and essentials. Canada's Lower-Risk Gambling Guidelines suggest no more than 1% of household income before tax per month. Treat the budget as spent the moment you deposit it.

Step 2: split it into sessions

A $100 monthly budget over four sessions gives you $25 per session. When a session's money is gone, the session ends. Don't borrow from next week.

Step 3: size your bets

A common rule is 1% to 2% of the session bankroll per bet on slots, so 25 cents to 50 cents a spin from $25. Higher stakes end sessions faster.

Step 4: set limits in your account

  • Deposit limit equal to your monthly budget.
  • Session time limit, with reminders every 30 minutes.
  • A loss limit per day or week, where offered.

Step 5: decide your win rule in advance

If you're ahead, decide before you play what you'll do, for example withdraw anything over your starting session amount. Winnings left in a casino balance tend to be played back.

Stop signals

  • You want to deposit again to win back losses.
  • You're playing longer to reach a loyalty level or clear a bonus.
  • You feel annoyed or anxious when you stop.

Any of these means stop for the day. If they keep happening, read our responsible gambling page.
